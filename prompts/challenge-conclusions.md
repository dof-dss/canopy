# Challenge Canopy conclusions

Critically review an interpretation of a Canopy result document.

Look for:

- claims not supported by the recorded evidence;
- static checks being presented as runtime proof;
- missing permissions or dependencies being mistaken for healthy behaviour;
- infrastructure readiness being confused with Drupal integration, indexing,
  or operational capacity;
- warnings being escalated to failures without a defined expectation;
- project differences being treated as defects without checking documented
  capability profiles or exceptions.

Return only material challenges and the additional evidence needed to resolve
them.
